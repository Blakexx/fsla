# `newton_row`

Mirrors `measure/lab/experiments/newton_row.cu`, an experiment of the lab
([`docs/internals/tools/lab.md`](../internals/tools/lab.md)).

The producer's entmax threshold is solved on the projection's accumulators by Newton from the left. On a `wgmma` m64n256 accumulator's rows: how many steps does a row take, and a warp (its 16 rows' vote); what does a 64-token block cost; is the support EXACT against the fp64 sorted closed form?

THE FORM. A warpgroup holds a 64-token block of 256 logits a token in the m64n256 accumulator's layout ([`wgmma_regs`](wgmma_regs.md)), loaded before the clock; one or two warpgroups a CTA, a CTA an SM. THE MATHEMATICS. With a row shifted by its max (`x = z - max`), alpha 1.5's level is `((x - tau) / 2)_+^2` and alpha 2's `(x - tau)_+`; `f(tau) = sum level - 1` is convex and decreasing, and `f(-2) >= 0` at alpha 1.5 (`f(-1) >= 0` at 2: the max alone), so Newton from there never passes the root. At a bound `lo`, with `n`, `P` and `Q` the count, sum and sum of squares of the support `{x > lo}`: alpha 1.5's `f(lo) = (Q - 2 P lo + n lo^2) / 4 - 1` and `f'(lo) = -(P - n lo) / 2`, so its step is `lo + (Q - 2 P lo + n lo^2 - 4) / (2 (P - n lo))`; the closed form on that support is alpha 1.5's smaller root of `n tau^2 - 2 P tau + Q - 4`, alpha 2's `(P - 1) / n` (the step itself); it is the root exactly when no member of the support lies below it, the count of `x >= tau` equal to `n`. Each step is a pass over a thread's 128 values -- one compare and three predicated adds a value (no select), then the settled count's compare and predicated add -- and two shuffles a sum; the warp's vote over its 16 rows ends the loop, cap 16. The inputs are `tests/integration/test_production_levels.py`'s stress kinds at 256 columns (random, near the boundary, a collapsed support, five times the scale); the oracle the entmax package's sorted closed form (`entmax15`, `sparsemax`) in fp64. Every row's support, threshold and steps are written and checked.

PREDICTION (written 2026-11-27, before the first run). Steps (a float32 emulation of the same iteration on the host, 4,096 rows a kind): a row at most 7; a warp's mean, alpha 1.5: random 5.6, near the boundary 5.5, collapsed 1.0, five times 2.8; alpha 2: 4.3, 4.0, 1.0, 2.1 (each within 0.6). Support EXACT on every row, none capped; the threshold within 2e-6 of fp64. Cycles a 64-token block = 260 (the max and shift) + S c, S the warp's steps: one warpgroup a CTA c in [700, 1,000] (a step's pass is 128 values x 6 instructions, 768 a warp, plus the shuffles', divide's and vote's latency), two warpgroups c in [650, 900]. So alpha 1.5 random about 4.2K-6.6K cycles a block: a block's solve is 1 to 1.6 times its projection's MMAs at hidden 256 (16 k16 steps of 128 cycles, two warpgroups on one pipe: 4,096 cycles a 128-token tile).
