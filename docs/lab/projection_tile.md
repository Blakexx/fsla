# `projection_tile`

Mirrors `measure/lab/experiments/projection_tile.cu`, an experiment of the lab
([`docs/internals/tools/lab.md`](../internals/tools/lab.md)).

The producer's projection tile alone: a job is 128 tokens x 256 columns over the hidden axis, two math warpgroups of 64 tokens each issuing `wgmma` m64n256k16 from shared memory, one loader warp staging a K-step (64 hidden) of `x` [128 x 64] and the job's weights [256 x 64] by `cp.async` into a ring of three stages. What does a job cost at hidden 256, 512 and 2,048, against its tensor floor (16 x hidden cycles: two warpgroups' steps on one pipe, 128 cycles a step)?

THE FORM. A CTA an SM, each its own 128-token tile of `x`, every CTA the same four jobs' weights (a write head's, from L2); a job's K-loop then an epilogue folding the accumulators; the next job re-stages the tile (a write head's jobs, each re-reading its tile). The operands are small integers in bf16, so every sum is exact: the last job's accumulators of two CTAs are checked against the host's products.

PREDICTION (written 2026-11-28, before the first run). A K-step (64 hidden) a CTA: the tensor floor 1,024 cycles (8 steps x 128, two warpgroups on one pipe). Its 48 KB come through L2 at two of [`stream_rate`](stream_rate.md)'s rates past L1 (run 20261128T013030Z-737802-5ab0223a): the CTA's own `x` tile, 16 KB of distinct lines, at 30.95 bytes a cycle an SM, and the weights every CTA reads, 32 KB, at 42.38; taken to add on the SM's path, 16,384 / 30.95 + 32,768 / 42.38 = 529 + 773 = 1,302 cycles, so L2 binds and not the tensor pipe. At hidden 2,048 `x`'s 16 KB also come from HBM (`x`, 69 MB, re-read each job, overruns the 50 MB L2) at 12.99 bytes a cycle an SM, 1,261 cycles, under the L2 path's 1,302. A job is its K-steps at 1,302 plus its share of the ring's first fill (~500 over 4 jobs) and its end (the wait for all, the fold and the clear, ~400): hidden 256, 4 x 1,302 + 900 = 6,108 cycles, 1.49x its floor (band 1.34-1.64); hidden 512, 8 x 1,302 + 900 = 11,316, 1.38x (1.24-1.52); hidden 2,048, 32 x 1,302 + 900 = 42,564, 1.30x (1.17-1.43).
