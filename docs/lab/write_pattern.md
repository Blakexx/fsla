# `write_pattern`

Mirrors `measure/lab/experiments/write_pattern.cu`, an experiment of the lab
([`docs/internals/tools/lab.md`](../internals/tools/lab.md)).

At what rate does the H100 write the producer's outputs at its cell, and which part of their address pattern prices it? The producer's floor ([`produce.md`](../internals/produce/produce.md#floor)) prices its 323.0 MB at HBM's write rate as [`stream_rate`](stream_rate.md) measured it, each CTA one contiguous slice; the library's stores alone at the cell ([`produce_skeleton`](produce_skeleton.md)) wrote them at about two thirds of it.

THE FORM. One kernel, one store form, three address maps of the same bytes. The producer's grid (2,048 CTAs: a sequence's 128-token tile and a write head, tiles fastest) at its stores alone's occupancy (three CTAs an SM, held by reserved shared memory); two warpgroups a CTA, each its 64-token share, 16 bytes a store and 8 in flight a thread. A share's 78,848 bytes: four level-sides' 16 KB row tiles, two masked rows' 2 KB of words, four level-sides' 64 scales of 4 bytes, and 64 tokens' 128 bytes of values. THE SLICE writes a share's bytes as one contiguous run (the floor's form at the producer's grid); WHOLE SECTORS writes the producer's regions with each scale plane head-major, so a share's 64 scales are one 256-byte run and every 32-byte sector is whole; THE PRODUCER'S OWN writes its regions as [`api.md`](../api.md#tile-major) lays them out, the scales token-major over the heads, so each 32-byte sector of a scale plane is written in pieces by the four heads' CTAs. Every output byte and a guard past each is checked after a launch from a sentinel.

PREDICTION, the run to come (written 2026-11-28, before it ran; its basis: the floor prices the producer's 323.0 MB at HBM's write rate as stream_rate measured it, each CTA one contiguous slice: 3,170 GB/s, 101.9 us; the library's stores alone at the cell (produce_skeleton, run 20261128T130545Z-524606-99b4f497) took 161.4 us, ~2.0 TB/s, at the same grid and occupancy as these rows, and ncu on its traced twin read DRAM at 58% of its peak whether one CTA an SM or two (warps active 11% and 22%), 16.2 MB READ back during a launch that reads nothing, and L2 write sectors 1.68x DRAM's bytes. THE HYPOTHESIS, NOT A FINDING until these rows name the price: the scales' 4-byte words, token-major over the four heads, leave every 32-byte sector of the 4.2 MB of scales written in four pieces by four CTAs at different times, so the L2 fetches each partial sector from DRAM to merge it (131K sectors x 4 pieces x 32 bytes = 16.8 MB, the reads seen) and HBM turns its bus between those scattered reads and the write stream. So THE SLICE, the same bytes one run a share, writes at the floor's rate (102-115 us: 101.9 and the launch's last partial wave of the 5.2 at 396 CTAs); WHOLE SECTORS, every run 256 bytes or more and sector-whole, writes near it (104-122 us); THE PRODUCER'S OWN reproduces the stores alone (152-170 us). The rival: the ~8 regions a CTA writes cost HBM's page locality, which would leave WHOLE SECTORS with THE PRODUCER'S OWN (above 145 us) and the slice alone at the floor; a slice above 125 us would say the form itself (256 threads, 8 in flight, three CTAs an SM) does not reach HBM's write rate and name the store form):

- the producer's 323.0 MB at the cell, three CTAs an SM: a share's bytes one contiguous run: 102.00-115.00 us.
- the producer's 323.0 MB at the cell, three CTAs an SM: its regions, every sector whole: 104.00-122.00 us.
- the producer's 323.0 MB at the cell, three CTAs an SM: its regions as the producer lays them out: 152.00-170.00 us.
- the producer's 323.0 MB at the cell, three CTAs an SM: its regions as the producer lays them out: over the slice: 1.35-1.65 times.
